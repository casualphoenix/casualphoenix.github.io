Health Companion privacy policy

Last updated November 5, 2026.

Health Companion is a personal Android app used only by its owner.

What the app reads

With the user's permission, the app uses read-only access to the Google Health API for activity, heart and other health measurements, sleep, ECG records and irregular rhythm records. It also uses the Google account's email address to keep the right account signed in.

Where data is kept

A copy of the health data is saved in the app's private storage on the user's phone so it can be shown offline. The app has no server of its own and sends this data nowhere except when requesting it from Google.

Goals, reminder settings, notes and check-offs stay on the phone. If the user turns on backup, those items, but not the Google Health readings, are also saved to a file the user chooses, such as one in their own Google Drive.

What the app doesn't do

The app doesn't sell or share data, show ads, use analytics, or use the data to train AI models. It can't change anything in Google Health.

Removing access and data

Access can be removed at any time from the Google account's connected apps page. Uninstalling the app deletes its data from the phone. A backup file can be deleted from wherever the user saved it.
